Insights of Visualization of Superstore Sales

1)Money Sales Trend (Top right- Line chart)
Sales steadily increased over the year with notable peaks in June, October, and December indicating seasonal variations.

2)Sales by Region and Category (Horizontal Bar Chart)
The Central Region dominates in terms of sales especially in the Technology category.

3) Donut chart for Sales and Profit
Technology contributes the most profit followed by Office Supplies, Furniture.
Technology not only brings in the most sales but also generates the highest profit margin.

4)Sales by Sub-Category (Tree- Map)
Phones, Chairs, Copiers, Bookcases, and Storage are the top-performing sub-categories.
